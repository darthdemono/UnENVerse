# Phase logs

The working record of every development session, in order: what was asked, what was built and why, the alternatives that were rejected, the bugs found, and how each change was checked. They are the long form behind the short [phase summaries](../index.md) and the [decision records](../../adr/README.md).

Lines about the maintainer's own machines, paths and accounts are left out; nothing else is edited except links between logs.
